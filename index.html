Evidence grades

Strong
Meta-analysis or several RCTs pointing the same way.
Moderate
At least one randomised or controlled trial, or a meta-analysis of mixed-quality studies.
Preliminary
Small, open-label, case-control or single-session studies.
Contested
Blinded, sham-controlled or pooled data that fail to support the effect, or a marker that does not hold up diagnostically.

    About this map

    Group-level EEG differences are not diagnostic tests for individuals. Summaries were compiled from the published literature and are a starting point, not a clinical guide. Check every claim against the original paper before using it with clients. Old 10-20 names are used: T3/T4/T5/T6 are T7/T8/P7/P8 in the 10-10 system.
